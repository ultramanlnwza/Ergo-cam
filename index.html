Camera setup

  1. Put the iPad on a stable stand about 2 m (6 ft) from the student.
  2. Point it at the student's side (a true side view, not front-on).
  3. Keep the ear, shoulder and hip on the camera-facing side in view, in good light.
  4. Tap Start Monitoring and choose Allow when Safari asks for the camera.

Camera & privacy

The camera is used only for live posture analysis. All processing happens on this device. Raw video and images are not stored or uploaded by this application. The camera is requested only after you tap Start Monitoring and is switched off when you stop.

Please note

This is an educational prototype, not a clinically validated medical device. Angles are 2D estimates from a single camera. The 35° threshold is a configurable prototype value that requires ergonomic validation; it is not an established medical diagnostic cut-off.
